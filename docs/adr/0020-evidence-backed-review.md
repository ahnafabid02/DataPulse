# Evidence-backed hospital review and complete clinical coverage

Accepted 2026-10-08 after the user's review of Q1-Q9. Expand ADR-019 through
reproducible fictional clinical seeds and bounded hospital-side profiling, followed
by M5 commands for human review and immutable source-specific assemblies/releases.
LLMs may use bounded column data to suggest relationships; name/value similarity
alone never authorizes a join. Deterministic uniqueness, unmatched-key and cardinality
checks support an identified human's decision. Incomplete evidence stays unresolved
until investigation supplies adequate documented evidence and checks.

Patient is the first complete review/release slice, followed by encounters,
observations and every other discovered clinical domain. Every source column must
stay visible in a coverage inventory: mapped/proposed/unresolved/rejected or explicitly
outside clinical scope with a reason. Operational exclusions require review; staged
releases declare their exact coverage and never imply complete interoperability.
Approval binds an exact immutable digest; edits require fresh approval. One identified
human reviewer is sufficient for the trusted local demo. Review files/commands remain
hospital-local; visual integration is M8, transformation/ingestion is M6.

Implement the smallest coherent prerequisite first: fictional seeds, bounded evidence
capture, immutable local observations and access audit. These are not join approval,
patient linking, extraction cursors, executable releases or full FHIR validation.
